03 // ENGINEERING CASE STUDY // [RESEARCH_LOG]

CHALLENGES BEHIND BUILDING A HUMANOID ROBOT

An interactive technical deep-dive into the complex physics, hardware constraints, and algorithmic hurdles of human-centered cybernetics.

CASE STUDY // LOG-01

Motor Alignment & Gear Backlash

THE CHALLENGE

Achieving ultra-precise mechanical alignment of high-torque brushless motors with zero gear backlash across 32 joint axes.

WHY IT IS DIFFICULT

Microscopic angular misalignments create mechanical resonance, rapid gear tooth wear, thermal spikes, and jitter in high-frequency trajectory execution.

HUMORPHIC SOLUTION

We engineer custom optical magnetic encoders with 18-bit resolution and CNC-machined zero-backlash harmonic strain wave gear housing inserts.

04 // CHRONOLOGICAL MILESTONES // [ROADMAP]

OUR DEVELOPMENT JOURNEY

From initial mathematical theory and kinematic simulation to fully articulated physical humanoid prototype.

01
PHASE 01 // FOUNDATIONS

Concept & Research

Formulated biomorphic movement equations, kinematic balance physics, and core system specifications for our 32-DOF humanoid platform.

02
PHASE 02 // CAD MODELING

CAD & Designing

Engineered full 3D parametric CAD models in SolidWorks, completing FEA structural stress and mass moment of inertia analyses on all joints.

03
PHASE 03 // FABRICATION

Parts Manufacturing

Precision CNC machined aircraft-grade 7075 aluminum joints, molded carbon fiber shells, and custom multilayer power distribution PCBs.

04
PHASE 04 // STRUCTURAL BUILD

Mechanical Assembly

Built out the physical 32-DOF articulated skeleton, integrating harmonic strain wave gearboxes, bearings, and internal wiring channels.

05
PHASE 05 // CALIBRATION

Motor Alignment

Aligned 18-bit absolute optical magnetic encoders across all joint axes, performing zero-offset indexing and thermal torque testing.

06
PHASE 06 // CYBERNETICS

Electronics Integration

Wired 8Mbps CAN-FD real-time control bus network, connected 700Wh smart Li-Ion battery pack, and flashed RTOS micro-kernel motor drivers.

07

Software & Control

Deployed Whole-Body Control (WBC) balance loops at 1,000 Hz, stereo depth point-cloud transformers, and real-time path planners.

08
PHASE 08 // VALIDATION

Testing & Improvements

Conducted 3,200+ empirical walking trials, terrain disturbance recovery tests, dynamic push recovery, and continuous optimization.
